Custom Jewelry

How Your Jewelry Is Made

From the first meeting to the moment you first wear it — we guide you through every step.

01

Consultation

We meet in person at the atelier or online. We discuss your story, vision, style, budget, and any deadline you need to meet.

02

Design & 3D Visualization

We create sketches and a precise 3D model of the piece, which you can review and refine with us before production begins.

03

Selecting the Diamond & Stones

Together we select a certified diamond or gemstone to your exact criteria — cut, color, and clarity.

04

Handcrafting

The piece is handmade in our Bratislava atelier, with careful attention to stone setting and clean lines.

05

Quality Control & Polishing

Every piece undergoes thorough quality control and final hand-polishing before it's ready for delivery.

06

Delivery & Aftercare

You receive your piece in person, along with a certificate of authenticity. From then on, we care for it with you — for life.
